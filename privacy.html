Recipe Friend

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Recipe Friend is a small, free recipe app run by an individual as a personal project. This policy explains exactly what it stores, why, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What we collect

From Google, when you sign in. Recipe Friend uses Sign in with Google so that there are no passwords to manage. We receive your name, your email address, and the account identifier Google issues. We do not request or receive access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar, or anything else in your Google account.

What you put into the app. The recipes you write or import — names, descriptions, ingredients, steps, tags, timings, and any photos you attach — together with the names of your recipe books and who belongs to them.

Your settings. Your measurement preferences (for example grams versus ounces), stored against your profile so they follow you between devices.

That is the whole list. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no third-party cookies. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not track you across other websites.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to sync your recipes between your devices, and to show your name to the other members of any recipe book you share. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising or marketing.

Where it is stored

Your account and recipes are stored in a hosted PostgreSQL database provided by Supabase, which also handles authentication and stores the photos you attach. The website itself is static and served by GitHub Pages. Sign-in is provided by Google. These providers process data on our behalf so the app can function; each has its own privacy policy.

Photos are served from web addresses that contain random identifiers. They are not listed or searchable anywhere, but anyone given the exact address of a photo can view it — the same bargain a share link makes. Please bear that in mind for anything you would not want seen outside the book it lives in.

Your browser also keeps a local copy of your recipes so the app loads instantly and works offline. That copy lives only on your device and you can remove it by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Shared recipe books

A recipe book can be shared with other people by invitation. Anything you put into a shared book — recipes, photos, edits — is visible to every member of that book, and they can edit or delete it. Your display name is visible to people you share a book with. Books you have not shared are visible only to you; this is enforced by database access rules, not merely by the app.

Share links

When you share a single recipe as a link, the recipe is encoded into the part of the URL after the #. Browsers never send that part to a server, so a shared recipe travels directly from you to whoever you send it to. We never see it. Anyone holding the link can read that recipe, so treat links as you would the recipe itself.

Keeping it, and deleting it

Your recipes are kept until you delete them or ask for your account to be removed. You can delete any recipe in the app at any time, and you can export everything as a file whenever you like.

To have your account and its data deleted, ask via the contact below. We will delete your profile, the books you own along with their recipes, and your membership of any shared book. Recipes you added to a book owned by someone else remain with that book, because they are part of a collection that belongs to its owner. Deletion is permanent, so export first if you want a copy.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit, sign-in is handled by Google so we never see a password, and access to every table is restricted by row-level security rules so that people can only reach books they belong to. No system is perfectly secure, and this is a personal project rather than a company with a security team — please bear that in mind when deciding what to store.

Children

Recipe Friend is not directed at children and should not be used by anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes to how your information is used will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

For privacy questions or a deletion request, open an issue at github.com/malfernion/Recipe-friend/issues, or use the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.
